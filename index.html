BalanceLens Privacy Policy

Know where your money goes.

Effective date: October 7, 2026
App: BalanceLens (com.frappstudio.balancelens)
Developer: FR App Studio

BalanceLens does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the BalanceLens Android app ("the app") handles information. BalanceLens is an offline personal money tracker that lets you record income and expenses, view monthly totals and understand your spending by category.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The app has no user accounts, no sign-up and no servers. We do not receive your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers or financial data.

3. Information you enter in the app

The transactions you add (type, category, amount, note and date) are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. This data is not sent to the developer or to any third party.

4. Permissions

PermissionStatus
InternetNot requested. The app works fully offline.
Location, camera, microphone, contacts, storageNot requested.

5. Third-party services

The app does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services, and does not integrate third-party SDKs that collect data.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your data with anyone.

7. Device backup

Android may include app data in your device's backup to your own Google account if you have device backup turned on. This is controlled by your device and your Google account settings, not by the developer, and the developer cannot access those backups.

8. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you remove it. You can delete individual transactions inside the app. To remove all data, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.

9. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot normally access. Because the data stays on your device, its protection also depends on your device security, such as a screen lock.

10. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
